Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version

  • HasGym has no accounts and no servers. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data.
  • Everything you log is stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in Apple Health if you allow it.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

1. Who we are

HasGym is a workout timer and fitness tracker for iPhone, Apple Watch and iPhone Duo. This policy covers the HasGym app and this website. You can reach us at hasgymappsupport@gmail.com.

2. Data the app uses

The app works with the data below. All of it stays on your devices. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

DataWhat it is used forWhere it is kept
Timers and settingsYour saved timers, sounds, voice cues, themes and other preferencesOn your device
Workout historyThe date, type, length and sets of each workout you run, shown in ProgressOn your device
ExercisesThe exercises, weights, reps and sets you log, used for your charts and Strength ScoreOn your device
MealsThe meals you log, with the scores and notes you give themOn your device
Health dataSee section 3Apple Health and your device

HasGym does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

3. Health data

Health access is optional. HasGym asks for it only if you turn on Live Health Stats or save workouts to Apple Health, and the app works without it.

With your permission, HasGym:

  • Reads your heart rate, active energy (calories) and workouts from Apple Health, to show them live during a workout, on your workout summary and in your history.
  • Writes the workouts you complete to Apple Health, with their heart rate and active energy.

We do not use health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we never disclose it to third parties. HasGym itself does not store health data in iCloud. Workouts and heart rate that HasGym saves to Apple Health are kept by Apple Health, and Apple may sync them to iCloud if you have turned on iCloud for Health in your iPhone settings. Heart rate zones, calories and Strength Score are estimates, not medical measurements.

4. Apple Watch

If you use the HasGym Apple Watch app, your iPhone and Apple Watch send each other the running timer, your settings and your live heart rate and calories. This happens directly between your own devices.

5. Notifications, sounds and Live Activities

Timer alerts, voice cues and Live Activities are created on your device. HasGym does not use a push notification server.

6. Purchases

Themes and HasGym Pro are bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your payment details, and the app only learns whether a purchase is active.

7. Third parties

The app shares no data with third parties. It contains no advertising networks, analytics tools or third-party SDKs. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and require any third party to give your data the same protection described here.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete workouts, exercises, meals and saved timers in the app.
  • Deleting HasGym from your iPhone removes all the data the app stored on that device.
  • Workouts saved to Apple Health stay in Apple Health. You can delete them in the Health app.
  • You can withdraw health access at any time in Settings ▸ Health ▸ Data Access & Devices ▸ HasGym, and notification access in Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ HasGym.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete or send you on request. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, the app's data is part of that backup, under your Apple account.

9. Children

HasGym is rated 16+ on the App Store and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

10. This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives the technical information any website visit sends, such as your IP address. The site loads nothing from any other company. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hasgymappsupport@gmail.com.
